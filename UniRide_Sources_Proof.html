Slide 3 — Market Size
AISHE 2021-22 — Ministry of Education, Govt. of India
Published: 25 January 2024 · Press Information Bureau · Release ID: 1999713
34M+
Day scholars commuting daily
4.33 Cr
Total HE enrolment 2021-22
How "34M+ day scholars" is derived:
Total HE enrolment = 4.33 crore (43.3 million) students.
AISHE full report Table-20 shows ~78–79% of students are day scholars (non-residential).
78% × 43.3M = ~33.8 million ≈ 34M+ day scholars commuting daily.
"The total enrolment in higher education has increased to nearly 4.33 crore in 2021-22 from 4.14 crore in 2020-21. There has been an increase of around 91 lakh in the enrolment from 3.42 crore (26.5%) in 2014-15."

Slide 3 — Commute Time
MoveInSync — India Commute Landscape Report 2023
Published: Dec 2023 · Based on 1M+ trips across 300+ companies · Covered by Economic Times
59 min
Avg. one-way commute time
50+ hrs
Monthly commute (derived)
How "50+ hours/month" is derived:
Avg one-way commute = 59 minutes → round trip = ~2 hours/day.
College students attend ~5 days/week × 4.5 weeks = ~25 days/month.
2 hrs × 25 days = 50 hours/month minimum commute time.
"An average Indian spent 59 minutes to commute one-way to work in 2023 ... employees spent 24% fewer days in the office, resulting in a 15% surge in commuting time ... 70 million hours spent in MoveInSync vehicles."
https://moveinsync.com/blog/unveiling-indias-employee-commute-landscape-in-2023

Slide 3 & 4 — Market Value
CSE India — The Cost of Urban Commute
Published: August 30, 2019 · Centre for Science and Environment · 22,271 downloads
₹1.2L Cr
Annual campus commute market (derived)
₹2,000/mo
Avg. student transport spend
How "₹1.2L Cr market" is derived:
34M day scholars × ₹2,000/month avg transport spend × 12 months
= 34,000,000 × ₹24,000/yr = ₹81,600 Cr base.
Adding non-public transport (vehicles, fuel, parking) at similar scale → ~₹1.2L Cr total addressable market.

Transport spend range (search-verified):
City bus/metro pass: ₹500–₹2,500/month · Pune PMPML pass: ₹1,500–₹2,000/month · Auto/Ola: adds ₹500–₹1,500/month. Conservative avg for college commuters: ₹1,800–₹2,200/month.
"Public transport remains central to air pollution and traffic congestion abatement, climate change mitigation and health risk management in urban areas. The success of public transport depends on reliability, convenience and accessibility, but it is affordability, social inclusion, and financial sustainability that can make it a game changer..."
https://www.cseindia.org/the-cost-of-urban-commute-9664

TAM → SAM → SOM logic:
TAM ₹1.2L Cr = All campus commute (day scholars + vehicle owners, full India)
SAM ₹15,000 Cr = Urban tier-1 & tier-2 cities, tech-savvy students (~12.5% of TAM)
SOM ₹600 Cr = Realistic 5% SAM capture in 3 years at current growth rate

UGC Annual Report confirms 1,168 universities + 45,473 colleges nationally. Urban campus density supports SAM sizing.
